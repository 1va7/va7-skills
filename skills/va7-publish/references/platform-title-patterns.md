# Platform Title Patterns

Use this reference when preparing text titles, body copy, and Hashtag for 小红书, 抖音, or 视频号.

## Shared Logic

The cover title and text title are a pair:

- Cover title: visual click driver.
- Text title: indexing and recommendation metadata.

When the cover is already punchy, use the text title to add searchable terms. When the cover is abstract, use the text title to clarify the domain and audience.

Recommended title formula:

```text
[domain keyword] + [specific entity/problem] + [question/claim]
```

Examples:

- `AI转型的真实成本：一天10亿token贵吗？`
- `AI创业者，为什么别买英伟达？`

## 小红书

Purpose: search indexing plus feed click.

Write titles that look like real user questions or concrete experience notes. Put high-value keywords in the title, body opening, and topic tags.

Good patterns:

- `AI转型的真实成本：一天10亿token贵吗？`
- `早期AI创业者该不该买英伟达？`
- `企业上Agent，token账单到底会涨到多少？`

Avoid:

- Pure slogans with no searchable noun.
- Repeating the cover title without adding domain keywords.
- Over-marketing phrasing.

Body:

- First sentence states the searchable problem.
- Use `#关键词[话题]#` tags in the body.
- Keep paragraphs short.

Hashtag:

- 5-8 tags.
- Include broad domain plus specific entity: `#AI转型 #Agent #token消耗`.

## 抖音

Purpose: stop, completion, comments.

Text title should match the first 3 seconds of the video. Use stronger curiosity, contradiction, or consequence, but keep keywords visible.

Good patterns:

- `每天10亿token，AI公司钱都花哪了？`
- `为什么AI创业者别急着买英伟达？`
- `企业上Agent后，最贵的不是模型调用`

Avoid:

- Long explanatory titles.
- Titles that promise a different topic than the opening hook.

## 视频号

Purpose: credibility and forwarding inside WeChat social graph.

Use more professional, shareable framing. The title should make sense when seen through a friend's like, group share, or public-account connection.

Good patterns:

- `AI转型的真实成本：token账单怎么看？`
- `早期创业者的钱，应该投给谁？`
- `企业Agent落地，如何判断token花得值不值？`

Avoid:

- Excessive shock or platform-native slang.
- Titles that are too narrow for public-account audiences.

## Body And Hashtag Checklist

Before writing:

- Identify cover title text.
- Identify 3-6 keywords the algorithm should associate with this video.
- Identify the script's core claim.
- Identify target audience: founder, operator, cross-border seller, AI builder, employee, etc.

Body structure:

1. Search frame: what problem this answers.
2. Evidence or structure: data, categories, story conflict, or framework.
3. Core claim: what the viewer should remember.
4. Topic tags.

Hashtag structure:

```text
#Domain #Entity #Scenario #Audience #Method
```

Chapters are separate platform metadata. They should not duplicate the full body. Use short section labels and concrete summaries:

```text
【标题不超14字】
00:01-00:56
内容不超20字
```
